The science

Echoes

Sound travels about 343 metres every second and bounces off hard rock, so a hello into a cave 170 metres deep comes back one second later; in caves shallower than about 17 metres, it returns too fast to hear apart from your own voice.

Ask: where else have you heard your voice come back?
