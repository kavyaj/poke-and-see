World Globe

This is the real Earth, photographed from space and lit the way the Sun lights it right now, so wherever it's dark, people are sleeping.

Ask: where does someone you love live? Can you find it?

Earth photos: NASA Blue Marble Next Generation and Earth at Night. Borders, ocean, sea and lake names: Natural Earth. Flags: flag-icons. Some places are territories of another country, and names follow common English usage. The pin button guesses your side of the world from your device's time zone. It doesn't know where you are.
